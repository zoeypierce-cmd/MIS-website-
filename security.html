Malware and Cyber Threats

The ILOVEYOU Computer Worm

On May 4th, the email worm ILOVEYOU infiltrated millions of computers. It originated as an email with the subject line ILOVEYOU. Once the user clicked on it, it would replicate and steal passwords through a trojan program send them to servers in the Philippines. This worm affected millions of computers worldwide and caused around $5-8 billion dollars of damage. The significance is that it illustrates how social engineering can be used to spread malware, thus highlighting the need for strong cyber security measures.
